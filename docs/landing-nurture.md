## 2 — day three

Subject: the thought isn't the problem

Hi,

One thing nobody tells you about a looping thought: the thought isn't the problem. The
check is. The ask-again is. The one-more-search is.

Every time you do the thing that makes it go quiet, you teach it to come back louder.

Steady is built around that one idea. It will never tell you the door is locked. It will
never say you're fine. It sits with you while the thought sits there, and nothing happens.
That's the whole trick, and it's the one therapists have used for forty years.

Two minutes of questions and you're in: https://beingsteady.com/invite
