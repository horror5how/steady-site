# beingsteady.com: top 50 mobile changes for paid traffic

Ranked by expected impact on one number: the share of Meta and TikTok ad clicks that finish the nine-question application at /invite. Every line is tailored to what the live page and /invite said on 13 October 2026. Sources are videos whose transcript or caption was read on that date; the full list is in mobile-conversion-top-50.sources.md. "source: synthesis" means the point is my own reasoning from the live page, not from a video.

## The hero (what the thumb sees before it scrolls)

1. Send ad clicks to a stripped page (the /landing or /mobile route), not the homepage: no top menu, no "Log in", no "For therapists", one job only. source: build_in_public, "Conversion rate optimization hacks backed by real A/B tests" (Yuppiechef removed navigation, 3% to 6%), https://www.tiktok.com/@build_in_public/video/7693722781028011297

2. Give the hero one button, not two. Today "Talk to Steady now" and "Apply for a place" compete for the same tap; for ad traffic make "Apply" the only button and push the taster further down. source: Neil Patel, "The Anatomy Of A High Converting Landing Page", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hcPxMuxh5Tc

3. Check the live taster before spending a penny. The page still says "Talk to Steady now", but the OpenAI key behind Steady's voice was switched off on 8 October (law 25); I did not test it this session. If it does not answer, every visitor who taps it hits a dead end. source: synthesis

4. Make the headline repeat the ad. If the ad says "checking the lock again", the first words on the page say that too, in the visitor's words, not "A calm voice for a loud mind". source: Solutions 8, "From Ad Clicks to Customer Conversions", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DduvshWGXbA

5. Build one landing variant per ad angle (checking, asking for reassurance, 2am searching, Pure O thoughts) and only reuse an ad on a page that matches its desire, awareness and person. source: Nick Theriot, "How To Test Landing Pages With Facebook Ads", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z2yX6GW511s

6. Open with a frustration hook in the shape "still doing X even though Y": "Still going back to check, even though you know it's locked?" source: Daniel Priestley, "The $1 Million Landing Page (Works For Normal People)", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az1Zh-FNSno

7. Sub-headline says how long, that it's free, and one benefit: "Nine questions, two minutes, free. If it fits, you're talking to Steady tonight." source: ScoreApp, "9 Quiz Landing Page Tips to Increase Leads and Conversions", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-rJZ36pVE8E

8. Button copy names the first step, not a vague verb: "Start my 2-minute check" beats "Apply" or "Get started". source: Antoine Dupont, "3 Above the Fold Tactics To Skyrocket Conversions", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uOeTE5IlAY

9. At 375px wide, only four things sit above the fold: headline, sub-headline, button, one line of proof. Write the mobile copy separately, shorter, and set it bigger, not smaller, and say "tap", not "click". source: Malewicz, "The Secret to Mobile Web Conversion", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8yUIbRiNRc

10. Button 52 to 64 pixels tall, full width, easy to hit with a thumb; no tiny trust icons that can't be read on a phone. source: Malewicz, "The Secret to Mobile Web Conversion", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8yUIbRiNRc

11. Keep the risk line right under the button as tick marks: free, no card, two minutes, adults 18+, not therapy. One line, readable size. source: Arsh Sanwarwala (ThrillX), "Alex Hormozi's Landing Page Strategy for 2026" (badges under the button, 30% lift), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zA0B-VwOPn4

12. Swap the "phone inside a phone" style mock-up (the "Practice preview" card) for one small visual of the loop itself: the door, the check, the doubt coming back. source: Malewicz, "The Secret to Mobile Web Conversion", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8yUIbRiNRc

13. The hero image should be someone the visitor recognises as themselves: an ordinary adult, late at night, phone in hand. Not a model, not an abstract shape. source: Antoine Dupont, "3 Above the Fold Tactics To Skyrocket Conversions", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uOeTE5IlAY

14. Spend most of the effort above the fold and test the headline and the image first; Hormozi says 90% of his tests are one of those two. source: Alex Hormozi, "3 Landing Page Tests To Skyrocket Conversions & Optins", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Dc2UQbQNTo

## Cut the page down

15. Shrink the ad page to roughly a tenth of the homepage (which runs to about 1,500 words): headline, sub-headline, three bullets, button. Hormozi uses three layouts, and all are that short. source: Alex Hormozi, "Here's how to build landing pages that convert", https://www.tiktok.com/@ahormozi/video/7301057834349759787

16. A landing page is not the website. Say what it is, give the reasons, take the action; anything the visitor has to scroll through to find the button loses them. source: zutrue, "High Converting Landing Page vs Low Converting Landing Page Design", https://www.tiktok.com/@zutrue/video/7455139873016515886

17. Every bullet must be worth the tap on its own; buyers decide on one issue, so cut the rest. Keep "It won't reassure you" and "talking tonight, no waiting list"; cut anything that only describes features. source: MoreMozi, "Your Sales Page Is Too Complicated", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4rbx2gzJzi4

18. "How it works" stays at three steps (Find your words, Understand the loop, Choose your next step); four is the ceiling, because more steps feel like more effort. source: Arsh Sanwarwala (ThrillX), "Alex Hormozi's Landing Page Strategy for 2026", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zA0B-VwOPn4

19. Write every section heading so it carries the point alone. "WHAT STEADY ACTUALLY DOES" becomes "Say the thought out loud. Watch it lose its grip." Most people only read headings. source: Arsh Sanwarwala (ThrillX), "Alex Hormozi's Landing Page Strategy for 2026", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zA0B-VwOPn4

20. No scroll hijacks, no decorative animations, no sticky header on mobile; they slow the page and annoy a visitor who can leave with one swipe. source: Malewicz, "The Secret to Mobile Web Conversion", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8yUIbRiNRc

21. After the first scroll, show a slim bottom bar with the apply button so the next step is always one tap away. source: Solutions 8, "From Ad Clicks to Customer Conversions", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DduvshWGXbA

22. End the page with the same button again; never make someone scroll back up to apply. source: Alex Cattoni, "This Landing Page Has A 79% Conversion Rate. Here's Why It Works", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vC_LEc8pGp8

23. Retarget everyone who opened /invite but did not finish, with an ad that sends them back to the application, not the homepage; one funnel builder retargets quiz drop-outs as a standard step. source: brenhammel, "6 Figure Per Month Quiz Funnel", https://www.tiktok.com/@brenhammel/video/7174050098177920262

24. Lazy-load the voice widget and anything heavy after the first paint, so the headline and button arrive first on a 4G connection inside the Instagram or TikTok in-app browser. source: synthesis

## Proof and trust

25. Put the strongest proof you actually have right next to the button. Today that is the NICE line ("exposure practice, what NICE recommends first for OCD in adults"); keep it in the first screen. source: Solutions 8, "From Ad Clicks to Customer Conversions", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DduvshWGXbA

26. Never invent a testimonial. Ask early users, with consent, for one line each about what changed ("I stopped re-checking the hob"), not "great app". source: Chris Raroque, "How I Make Landing Pages For My Apps", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nn23ZVdu6_A

27. When real quotes exist, show them all at once in a stack, not in a swipe carousel; carousel content mostly goes unseen on mobile. source: Arsh Sanwarwala (ThrillX), "Alex Hormozi's Landing Page Strategy for 2026", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zA0B-VwOPn4

28. Add a short founder note: who built Steady and why. People back people, and it answers "is this a faceless AI company?" source: Chris Raroque, "How I Make Landing Pages For My Apps", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nn23ZVdu6_A

29. Lead with the unique mechanism, high up: Steady won't reassure you, because reassurance feeds the loop. That is what separates it from every chatbot in a crowded market. source: psychologyofads, "7 years of advertising advice in 5 minutes", https://www.tiktok.com/@psychologyofads/video/7494295947275422982

30. Use one "us versus them" line in the first two screens: "A chatbot will tell you the door is locked. Steady won't." source: theecomcoach, "4 of the best Facebook ads for Ecommerce stores", https://www.tiktok.com/@theecomcoach/video/7380345570864467218

31. Move the two biggest objections ("Is this just ChatGPT?" and "What does it cost?") up from the FAQ into short lines near the top; objections answered early convert better. source: Alex Cattoni, "This Landing Page Has A 79% Conversion Rate. Here's Why It Works", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vC_LEc8pGp8

32. Record a 30 to 45 second silent-captioned clip of a real Steady conversation and put it under the hero; a demo video is what pushes many visitors to sign up. source: Chris Raroque, "How I Make Landing Pages For My Apps", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nn23ZVdu6_A

## The nine-question application at /invite

33. Keep the first question about why they came ("What brings you here?" already does this) and make it multi-select, because most people arrive with more than one loop. Headspace saw 10% more trial starts from that change. source: Mobbin, "I Studied 1,460 Onboarding Flows. Here's What I Found.", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qsq-Sj_rojU

34. One question per screen with the "1 / 10" progress counter always visible; splitting a form across screens lifted one app's sign-ups by 15%. source: Mobbin, "I Studied 1,460 Onboarding Flows. Here's What I Found.", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qsq-Sj_rojU

35. Write the questions like a person talking, not a form; one conversational rewrite gave Dollar Shave Club 5% more subscriptions. source: Mobbin, "I Studied 1,460 Onboarding Flows. Here's What I Found.", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qsq-Sj_rojU

36. Ask for the email as late as possible, after they've answered the questions about themselves; convert first, collect details later. source: Malewicz, "The Secret to Mobile Web Conversion", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8yUIbRiNRc

37. Drop one short affirming screen after "How much room does it take up?": "That's a lot to carry. You're in the right place." source: Adam Lyttle, "The app onboarding secrets that convert (proven strategies)", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QMo2T5apdYw

38. Add one honest-expectation line before the end: the first practices feel uncomfortable, and that is the method working. Setting expectations early builds trust and cuts drop-off later. source: Adam Lyttle, "The app onboarding secrets that convert (proven strategies)", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QMo2T5apdYw

39. Put one privacy line in the flow just before the personal questions ("You can see and delete anything Steady remembers"), not only on a separate page. source: Adam Lyttle, "The app onboarding secrets that convert (proven strategies)", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QMo2T5apdYw

40. At the end, show what their answers unlocked: a first sketch of their loop (trigger, thought, response) built from their own words, before they enter the app. source: Mobbin, "I Studied 1,460 Onboarding Flows. Here's What I Found.", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qsq-Sj_rojU

41. People who don't qualify (under 18, crisis, wrong state) get a useful next step on the same screen, never a bare "no". The flow already has "There's something better for you"; make sure each one has a real link. source: Daniel Priestley, "The $1 Million Landing Page (Works For Normal People)", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az1Zh-FNSno

42. Keep "Anything you'd like us to know?" optional, and read every answer weekly; the words people type there are your next ad headlines. source: Daniel Priestley, "The $1 Million Landing Page (Works For Normal People)", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az1Zh-FNSno

43. The final screen tells them exactly what happens next and when ("You're in. Tap to start your first talk now"), not a generic thank-you. source: Solutions 8, "From Ad Clicks to Customer Conversions", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DduvshWGXbA

44. Treat the question count as a dial: if applications are too few, cut a question; if too many unsuitable people apply, add one. More steps, fewer but better applicants. source: Alex Hormozi, "Here's how to build landing pages that convert", https://www.tiktok.com/@ahormozi/video/7301057834349759787

45. Test the word "apply" against "check". "Check if Steady fits you" can feel less like a gatekeeper than "apply for a place". source: ScoreApp, "9 Quiz Landing Page Tips to Increase Leads and Conversions", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-rJZ36pVE8E

## Ads, targeting and measurement

46. The /invite flow asks "Which state are you in?", so only target ads at the places you can actually accept; a UK click that is turned away at question 7 is paid-for waste. source: synthesis

47. Write the page in the same specific language as the ad (checking, intrusive thoughts, OCD, rumination); Meta reads the landing page one level deep and uses it to decide who sees the ad. source: Nick Theriot, "How To Test Landing Pages With Facebook Ads", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z2yX6GW511s

48. Build three to five named personas (the checker, the reassurance-seeker, the Pure O thinker, the night-time ruminator) and match each page to their stage of awareness: problem-aware visitors need the loop named; solution-aware ones need the method named. source: psychologyofads, "7 years of advertising advice in 5 minutes", https://www.tiktok.com/@psychologyofads/video/7494295947275422982

49. Install Microsoft Clarity (free) on the ad page and /invite, watch 20 mobile recordings, then write one sentence: "most people drop at this spot". Fix that first. source: Solutions 8, "From Ad Clicks to Customer Conversions", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DduvshWGXbA

50. Until traffic passes about 40,000 visits a month, don't wait for split-test significance; ship bigger changes one at a time and judge them week on week, one change per week. source: Arsh Sanwarwala (ThrillX), "Alex Hormozi's Landing Page Strategy for 2026", https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zA0B-VwOPn4
